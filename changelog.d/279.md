### Pinned tabs in the horizontal tab bar and the vertical sidebar ([#279](https://github.com/filipac/runlet/issues/279))

- **The problem:** tabs you keep open all day, such as a scratch snippet, a production SQL tab,
  or a Redis tab, moved as other tabs opened and closed, and Close Other Tabs and Close Tabs to
  the Right closed them too.
- **Now:** any tab (PHP, SQL, Redis, MongoDB) can be pinned, in both tab layouts.
  - **Pin and unpin:** **Pin Tab** / **Unpin Tab** in the tab's context menu, and **Pin Tab** in
    the Window menu, the command palette, and Open Anything ("pin", "unpin"). Pinning runs
    nothing.
  - **Order:** pinned tabs come first. Pinning moves a tab to the end of the pinned tabs,
    unpinning to the start of the others. New tabs open after the pinned ones, a drag keeps a
    tab in its group, and ⌘1…⌘9 count the tabs as shown.
  - **Tab bar:** pinned tabs are compact: the tab kind's icon in its colour, a short title, and
    no close button, then a separator. The tooltip has the full title.
  - **Vertical tabs:** a **Pinned** section at the top, with one-line rows that show the
    selection, the edited dot, the run state, and the target's colour stripe.
  - **Closing:** Close Other Tabs and Close Tabs to the Right leave pinned tabs open, and are
    disabled when they would close nothing. ⌘W and Close in the menu still close a pinned tab,
    and Reopen Closed Tab brings it back pinned.
  - **Saved:** the pin is saved with the session and in `.runlet` workspaces. Sessions and
    workspaces saved before open unpinned, and a duplicated tab starts unpinned.
- Debug steps `pin`, `unpin`, `move-tab`, and `pins-state`; `scripts/pinned-tabs-screenshots.py`
  checks the rules end to end and takes the screenshots. See [docs/tabs.md](docs/tabs.md).
