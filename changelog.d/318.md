### Docker profiles: a click on a container always makes it the profile's container ([#318](https://github.com/filipac/runlet/issues/318))

- **The bug:** in **New Docker Profile** and the **Profiles** window, clicking a running container highlighted its row, but the form often kept "Container: None", an empty name, or an earlier container and user. Only some of what the click fills in reached the profile, often just the working directory, so saving could point the profile at the wrong container.
- **Now** a click always makes its container the profile's container, also on a row that was already highlighted, and the highlighted row and the profile's container always agree. Refresh and Docker coming back only highlight the profile's own container; they never pick one.
- **The fields a click fills in** (name, execution user, working directory, and local source) follow the latest click until you change them. A field you edited is kept; clearing it hands it back to the clicks.
- **Two profiles may use one container,** for example with different users or folders. The form says when another profile uses the chosen container.
