### Quick Run: a floating panel for a line of PHP, from any app ([#25](https://github.com/filipac/runlet/issues/25))

- **Quick Run** (**Window ▸ Quick Run**, the command palette, or Open Anything) opens a small floating panel above every app, like Spotlight: a PHP editor that grows with its lines, on the Laravel Sandbox unless you pick another target. ⌘R runs it, and nothing else does: opening the panel, typing, or restoring its code never runs anything.
- **The result shows in the panel:** the value as a tree (Eloquent models in the Values view), errors with their line, and printed output. Runs go through the same pipeline as a tab's, and History marks them with a bolt.
- **Open in Tab** (⌘↩) moves the code, its target, and its result into a new tab in Runlet's window, opening or bringing it forward. **Esc** closes the panel; it keeps its code, also after Runlet restarts.
- **Never on production:** the target menu leaves out production targets, and targets whose application said it runs in production. ⌘R checks again, so a target marked production while the panel is open is refused with the reason. Staging runs as usual.
- **A global shortcut,** off by default: **Settings ▸ Shortcuts ▸ Open Quick Run from any app**, ⌃⌥R unless you record another. It needs no Accessibility permission. Settings says when another app, macOS, or one of Runlet's own commands already uses the shortcut, and doesn't take it.
