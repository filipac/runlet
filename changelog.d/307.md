### Eloquent models show what they hold: Values | Object ([#307](https://github.com/filipac/runlet/issues/307))

- **Values:** an Eloquent model in a result, a dump, or a magic comment shows its class and key
  (`User #1`), its attributes, and its loaded relations, themselves in Values. Collections,
  paginators, and arrays of models show their models the same way, with the count
  (`Collection<User> · 312`) and a paginator's total and page. SQL and MongoDB models alike:
  ObjectId keys, embedded documents, and dates show as their values.
- **Marks:** an attribute that changed since the model was loaded has a dot (hover for the
  original value), the model says how many changed, and a model that isn't saved says **new**.
  Hidden attributes (`$hidden`, or left out by `$visible`) still show, marked hidden.
- **Object:** the **Values | Object** switch on result and dump cards shows the whole object, as
  before. It is the tab's choice, remembered with the tab, and covers its cards, Table, copies,
  Plain view, and inline values; **Open in Window** has a switch of its own. **Settings ▸
  General ▸ Output ▸ Show Eloquent models as** sets the default (Values).
- **Nothing runs:** Values reads only what the model holds, so casts, accessors, and appended
  attributes aren't applied, and no `toArray()`, getter, or `__toString()` runs. A driver's
  caster for a model class still decides how it shows, and runs once per object.
- **More rows:** Values leaves out what every model repeats, so a large collection fits: up to
  1,000 models in a list, within the same size limit. A model the limit would cut is left out
  whole, and the card, the Tree, and the Table say how many were left out. (A collection of 312
  models with nested attributes stopped at 198 before: their internals used up the value's
  budget.)
