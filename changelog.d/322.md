### Drag tabs to reorder them in the horizontal tab bar ([#322](https://github.com/filipac/runlet/issues/322))

- **The problem:** only the vertical tabs could be reordered by dragging. In the horizontal tab
  bar, tabs stayed in the order they were opened.
- **Now:** drag a tab left or right in the tab bar to move it, as in Safari and Xcode.
  - **While dragging:** the tab follows the pointer and is selected, and the tabs it passes
    slide aside to show where it will land. Release it to drop it there.
  - **Pinned tabs:** a pinned tab moves among the pinned tabs, and another tab among the others,
    as in the vertical tabs (#279). A tab dragged toward the other group stops at its own
    group's end.
  - **Many tabs:** hold a dragged tab near either end of the bar, and the bar scrolls.
  - **Clicks are unchanged:** a click selects a tab, a double-click renames it, and the close
    button and context menu work as before. Only a drag of a few points moves a tab, and
    dragging in a tab's rename field selects text.
  - **Saved:** the new order is saved with the session, and the vertical tabs show it too.
- **Move Tab Left and Move Tab Right:** new commands in the command palette (⇧⌘P) move the
  selected tab one place, inside its group. They have no shortcut; **Settings ▸ Shortcuts** can
  give them one.
